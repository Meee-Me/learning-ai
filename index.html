Why is 1/2 bigger than 1/3?

Picture one pizza.

  • Cut it into 2 pieces: each slice is 1/2.
  • Cut the same pizza into 3 pieces: each slice is 1/3.

More cuts means smaller slices, so 1/2 is bigger.
